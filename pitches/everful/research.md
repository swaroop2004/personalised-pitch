# Research: Everful (everfulwholesale.com)

## Company Overview
- **Name**: Everful (formerly Factory Price Jewelry)
- **Type**: B2B factory-direct sourcing platform for retailers & brands
- **Founded**: 2024 (launched as Factory Price Jewelry, rebranded to Everful)
- **Headquarters**: San Francisco, CA (9245 Laguna Springs Dr, Suite 203, Elk Grove, CA 95758) + Singapore
- **Employees**: ~70 globally (45 in R&D), AI operations team connecting engineering & business
- **Funding**: $48.5M Series A announced Nov 19, 2026
  - Lead: Vertex Ventures Southeast Asia & India, January Capital
  - Existing: Square Peg, Lightspeed
- **Revenue trajectory**: On track for $100M annualized revenue by end of 2026 (~2.5 years post-launch)

## Founders
- **Sisi Song** (Co-founder): Former Alibaba executive; former venture investor at Bessemer Venture Partners (focus: cross-border sourcing)
- **Kevin Han** (Co-founder): Serial entrepreneur; sold previous company to Amazon

## Product & Platform
- **Core offering**: Factory-direct wholesale marketplace connecting retailers/brands with 30,000+ vetted Chinese factories
- **Catalog**: 20M+ SKUs across jewelry, accessories, home, beauty, apparel, electronics, pet, outdoor, auto, tools; 10K+ new arrivals daily
- **Key differentiator (per founders)**: "We take care of everything. We handle sourcing, order consolidation, quality control, and logistics. That's something the marketplaces don't offer." — Kevin Han, TechCrunch Nov 19 2026
- **No minimums** on most products; mix MOQ as low as 10 units
- **Custom branding**: Logo engraving, custom product cards/tags, branded packaging
- **Private label / custom manufacturing**: Build products to retailer specifications
- **Dropshipping**: Custom product fulfillment direct to end customers
- **Quality control**: Every order hand-inspected before shipment
- **Shipping**: Free air shipping (eligible items), volume discounts up to 20%, VIP discounts up to 15%, tiered pricing up to 6% off
- **Global reach**: Ships 5M+ items/month to 60+ countries

## AI & Technology
- **AI agents** automate labor-intensive sourcing tasks:
  - Tracking consumer trends
  - Identifying suppliers
  - Coordinating orders across factories
  - Managing logistics
- **Trend analysis tool** reads three real-time signals:
  1. Search behavior (buyer/shopper intent)
  2. Marketplace demand (current order velocity)
  3. Category velocity (momentum across product categories)
- **AI operations team** connects engineering and business functions
- **Shopify integration** live; "deepening integrations with e-commerce platforms, building on its Shopify integration as it works toward becoming an end-to-end infrastructure platform for global sourcing" — TechCrunch
- **Mobile apps**: iOS (App Store) and Android (Play Store)

## Business Model & ICP
- **ICP**: Small-to-mid-size retailers, boutiques, brands without dedicated sourcing teams
- **Value prop**: Factory pricing without middlemen; end-to-end sourcing handled; no MOQ risk; AI-powered trend discovery
- **Revenue model**: Wholesale margin on products sold; potential platform/integration fees as they build infrastructure layer

## Recent Strategic Context (from Series A announcement)
- **Geopolitical awareness**: Building for "cross-border B2B sourcing plus local selling" model; de minimis exemption suspension ($800 duty-free) favors their approach
- **Series A deployment**: Expand US & Europe (UK, France, Italy); invest in AI to automate product customization & sourcing workflows; deepen e-commerce platform integrations (Shopify + others)
- **Competitive landscape**: vs. Alibaba (marketplace only), Faire (wholesale marketplace) — Everful differentiates on *operational responsibility* not just transaction facilitation

## Evidence of Developer/Platform Surface
- Shopify integration exists (public)
- Mobile apps with native API communication
- "End-to-end infrastructure platform for global sourcing" vision implies API/platform layer
- 45 R&D engineers building platform features
- No public `/developers`, `/docs`, `/api` section visible on marketing site
- Integration depth likely requires developer-facing documentation (API reference, webhook guides, auth flows, SDKs)
