## 1. Company Overview

Intake Breathing is a direct-to-consumer (DTC) nasal wellness brand selling a **patented magnetic nasal dilator system**. The product consists of:
- A reusable magnetic band (sizes S, M, L, XL)
- Disposable adhesive tabs (sold in refill packs)
- Complementary products: Intake Mouth Tape, Sport Tabs (extra-sticky for high-sweat activities)

**Positioning:** "Help everyone breathe better, sleep better, and get more out of life." Not a medical device — a wellness/performance product. Originally designed for motocross riders; now marketed for sleep, snoring, athletic performance, and everyday wellness.

**Founded:** ~2019–2022 (Kickstarter era; "hard years" hand-packing orders). Family-run: Jim Castillo (inventor), Dave (son), Natalie (daughter), Alex Hauck (son-in-law, CEO), Dan (next gen).

**HQ:** Santa Barbara, California (per funding announcement).

## 2. Product & Technology

| Product | Key Claims |
|---------|------------|
| **Intake Starter Kit** | 4 magnetic bands (S–XL) + 15 tab sets; 90-day risk-free trial |
| **Magnetic Nasal Dilator** | Two independent nasal strips + magnetic relief band; opens nasal valve from sides (vs. traditional strips that pull from bridge and collapse on inhale) |
| **Sport Tabs** | Extra-sticky adhesive for high-sweat / high-motion use (motocross origins) |
| **Intake Mouth Tape** | Complementary product for nasal breathing at night |
| **Refill Plans** | Subscription model for tab replenishment |

**Differentiators (per site):**
- Opens wider — magnetic lift expands from sides; traditional strips collapse inward on inhale
- Sweat-proof — designed for motocross; stays on through workouts and full nights
- Reusable band — only replace tabs (less waste, lower cost)
- Medical-grade — hypoallergenic, latex-free, FDA registered, made in USA
- Skin-safe adhesive — tested for overnight + sweaty wear

**Clinical Validation:** SleepScore Labs independent study (840+ nights analyzed):
- 88% of users reported reduction in snoring
- 92% reported relief from sinus pressure
- 87% reported deeper, more restful sleep

**Patents:** Multiple (linked in footer: https://help.intakebreathing.com/en-US/patents-368789)

## 7. Funding Context & Strategic Intent

**Investor:** Camino Partners — Daniel Lubetzky (KIND Snacks founder, Shark Tank). Portfolio: CAVA, Prose, Barry's. Thesis: "companies enhancing everyday lives, with growing emphasis on health and longevity."

**Camino's Operating Model:** Hands-on — "works hands-on with founders on strategy, operations and growth" (funding blog). Not passive capital.

**Stated Use of Funds (per Alex Hauck):**
1. **Growing the team** — "experienced operators across marketing, product, and operations who have scaled consumer brands before"
2. **Retail readiness** — "preparing operations, product lineup, and supply chain for order sizes that come with mass retail"; "smart retail partnerships that put Intake in front of people beyond the drugstore cold-and-flu aisle"
3. **Product expansion** — "becoming the home for all things breathing" — building on Mouth Tape and Sports Tabs

**Key Quote (Elle Lanning, Camino):** *"74% of Intake consumers have not been legacy category consumers. And that solidified it [the investment] for us."* — Modern Retail

**Analogy (Lanning):** Like KIND pulled health bars out of the supplement aisle into food, Camino wants to pull nasal breathing out of the cold/flu aisle into mainstream wellness.

| Competitor | Model | Notes |
|------------|-------|-------|
| **Breathe Right (GSK/Prestige)** | Single-use adhesive strips | Category leader; acquired by Prestige for $1B (2024); "cold-and-sinus aisle" incumbent |
| **Hostage Tape / ZzzTape** | Mouth tape | Adjacent; "mouth-taping craze" — Intake also sells mouth tape as complement |
| **Generic nasal strips** | Amazon private label | Low end; no magnetic/reusable system |
| **Prescription / Medical** | Turbinate reduction, CPAP | Clinical; not wellness |

**Intake's Moat:** Patented magnetic dilator (reusable band + tabs), sweat-proof, clinical validation, athlete/creator endorsements, provider network, brand positioning as "performance/wellness" not "sick care."
